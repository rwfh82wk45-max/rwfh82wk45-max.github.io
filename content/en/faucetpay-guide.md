FaucetPay is a custodial microwallet: it holds small crypto payments from faucets, PTC sites and similar services, and lets you withdraw them together. Here is how to set it up and get coins out without the usual mistakes.

## 1. Create a FaucetPay account

Sign up on the FaucetPay website with an email address you actually use, because many earning sites identify you by that email. Confirm the email, then turn on two-factor authentication in the security settings before you do anything else.

Use the website. Be careful with APK files or "FaucetPay app download" links from other sites, which are a common way to steal logins.

## 2. Find your FaucetPay email, username and wallet addresses

- **Email:** the one you registered with. Earning sites that pay to FaucetPay usually ask for it. SurfFlux, for example, requires a verified FaucetPay email before you can withdraw.
- **Username:** shown in your account profile.
- **Wallet addresses:** some sites ask for a deposit address instead of an email. In FaucetPay, open the deposit section, choose the coin, and you will see an address that belongs to your account for that coin.

Paste addresses, never retype them. One wrong character sends coins somewhere you cannot recover them from.

## 3. Link FaucetPay to the sites you use

Each site has its own field for this, usually in settings or on the withdrawal page. Enter the email or the address for the right coin. On SurfFlux, a site where you can [earn DOGE to FaucetPay](https://surfflux.com/), add and verify your FaucetPay email in Settings; withdrawals start at 0.009 DOGE.

## 4. Swap coins if you need to

FaucetPay lets you exchange between supported coins inside your balance. That is handy when you earn in several coins and want to withdraw in one.

## 5. How to withdraw from FaucetPay

1. Open the withdrawal section.
2. Pick the coin.
3. Enter or select the destination address.
4. Enter the amount, check the fee and the minimum for that coin.
5. Confirm, including the 2FA code.

Network confirmations can take minutes or longer depending on the coin.

## How to transfer from FaucetPay to Binance

Withdraw from FaucetPay to your Binance deposit address for the same coin. The important part is the **network**: on Binance, choose the network that matches the coin you are sending (for DOGE, the Dogecoin network). Copy the address from Binance, paste it into FaucetPay, and send a small amount first if you have never done this route before.

## FaucetPay to PayPal or USDT

FaucetPay is a crypto wallet and does not pay out to PayPal. To get to PayPal or a bank, you would send coins to an exchange that supports selling them in your country. USDT is one of the coins FaucetPay supports, so swapping to USDT first is a common way to avoid price changes while you move funds.
