Paid To Promote, or PTP, flips the usual earning model around. Instead of viewing ads yourself, you get a personal link and earn when other people open it and pass a short check. You are paid for the visitors you bring, not for your own clicks.

## How Paid To Promote sites work

1. You get a unique PTP link.
2. You share it anywhere visitors might click it: your blog, a forum signature, social media.
3. A visitor opens the link and lands on a short validation page.
4. If the visit qualifies, you earn a reward. If it looks automated or fraudulent, you earn nothing.

The site sells those validated visits to advertisers as traffic. That is why PTP sites care so much about quality: an advertiser who receives bot traffic stops buying.

## Promoting links versus promoting products

"Paid to promote" also gets used for influencer deals, where brands pay creators to post about a product. That is a different thing. PTP sites pay per qualifying visit to a link and have nothing to do with endorsing products.

## What makes a PTP visit count

Every site has its own rules, but they usually look at the same things:

- how long the page stays visible,
- whether the visitor is a real browser and not a script,
- where the visitor came from,
- whether the same person or network already visited.

Sending the link to traffic exchanges, autosurfs or bot services is the fastest way to get a PTP account banned.

## How SurfFlux PTP works

The [SurfFlux Paid To Promote program](https://surfflux.com/ptp) follows this model with a few specific rules (as of October 2026):

- A link is issued only after you accept the PTP terms.
- A visitor must keep the validation page actively displayed for at least 7 seconds.
- Your first 50 evaluable visits are a probation period and pay 3% of the corresponding advertiser rate. Very poor or non-human traffic pays nothing.
- Rewards are pending for 24 hours before they settle.
- Pay depends on the visitor's country tier and your traffic quality rating.

The visitors you send are then offered to advertisers as PTP traffic, which they can [buy as a campaign](/buy-website-traffic/).

## Is PTP worth it?

It is worth it if you already have a place where people click links: a blog in a related niche, an active forum profile or a community you run. It is not worth buying traffic to resell through PTP. Validation is designed to catch exactly that.

## Paid to promote sites: what to check

- Clear published rules on what counts as a valid visit.
- A probation or quality system, which shows the site takes fraud seriously.
- A low withdrawal minimum, so you can test whether it pays.
- An honest description of where the traffic goes.
