FeelingSurf is a free traffic exchange that has been running for a long time and is often recommended as a HitLeap replacement. Here is how it works and when you might want something different. We are not affiliated with FeelingSurf; this site is published by the SurfFlux team.

## How FeelingSurf works

- **Viewer.** You run the FeelingSurf viewer, which displays other members' sites and earns you credits.
- **Credits.** Credits are spent on visits to your own sites. You can also buy credits instead of earning them.
- **Settings.** Public listings mention geolocation targeting, adjustable visit duration and simulated clicks, plus a screenshot feature to check how your page displays.

FeelingSurf says it has exchanged billions of hits since 2007, which gives a sense of how long it has been around.

## Strengths

- Free to start.
- Long history and a large member base.
- Useful campaign settings for an exchange.

## Limits

- Viewer-based visits are largely unattended.
- Low engagement and conversion, as with every exchange.
- Not suitable for pages with AdSense.

## When a FeelingSurf alternative makes sense

**You want your earnings in crypto.** FeelingSurf rewards you with credits. If you would like the option to withdraw, an exchange paying in DOGE gives you that choice.

**You want visits through real browser tabs.** Some exchanges open campaigns in normal tabs via an extension, or as PTC ads that a person opens and confirms.
