Paid to click, usually shortened to PTC, is one of the oldest ways to make money online without skills or startup costs. It is also one of the least lucrative. A PTC site pays you a small amount to open an advertiser's page and keep it open for a few seconds.

## How a PTC site works

1. An advertiser buys a number of visits to a page, with a set viewing time, say 10 or 30 seconds.
2. The PTC site shows that ad in a list to its members.
3. You click it, the page opens, and a timer runs.
4. When the timer ends you confirm, often with a simple check, and the reward is added to your balance.

The site keeps the difference between what the advertiser pays and what you receive. That is the whole business.

## What a click is worth

Not much. On classic PTC sites paying in dollars, a click is usually worth a fraction of a cent. Crypto PTC sites pay in small coin amounts instead. On SurfFlux in October 2026, a 10-second ad paid roughly 0.0035 to 0.0087 DOGE depending on the campaign. Longer ads pay more, and so do ads targeted at your country.

## Paid to click: legit or scam?

The model is legitimate. Individual sites are another matter. The PTC space has a long history of sites that collapse after a year, often after promising too much. A few warning signs:

- Rates that seem high compared with other sites.
- "Investment" upgrades that promise to multiply your earnings. These have more in common with pyramid schemes than with advertising.
- Long waits for "pending" payments.
- Most ads are for other PTC sites rather than real businesses.

## How to find PTC sites that pay

- Start with a low withdrawal minimum and withdraw the first time you can.
- Read recent reviews, from the last month or two.
- Prefer sites where you can see what an advertiser pays. If advertising prices are public, you can check whether the payouts are plausible.
- Avoid apps that exist only as an APK download.

## PTC apps, PTC jobs and other variations

You will see "paid to click jobs" and "paid to click apps" promoted as work. They are the same thing: small rewards for views, sometimes mixed with surveys and app installs. Calling it a job does not change the pay.
