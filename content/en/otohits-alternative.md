Otohits is a free autosurf traffic exchange. This page explains how it works and when you might prefer something else. We are not affiliated with Otohits; this site is published by the team behind SurfFlux.

## How Otohits works

You add your websites, then run the Otohits viewer, either as an app or a browser extension. The viewer opens other members' sites automatically and earns you points, which are spent on visits to your own sites. According to independent overviews, Otohits lets you add unlimited sites, use timers of roughly 10 to 60 seconds, hide the referrer, and place adult content in a separate category. It is free to use.

People search for the "Otohits app" and the "Otohits viewer" because that program is the whole system. As long as it runs, you earn points.

## Strengths

- Free, with no limit on the number of sites.
- Runs on its own, so it needs no attention.
- Flexible timers.

## Limits

- Autosurf traffic is mostly unattended, so engagement is low.
- Independent reviews describe the traffic quality as low, and it is not much use for video views.
- Like all exchange traffic, it must not go to pages with AdSense.

## When an alternative makes more sense

**You want something back other than points.** Otohits rewards you with visits. If you would rather earn cryptocurrency, an exchange that pays in DOGE lets you choose between promoting your own site and withdrawing.

**You want a person to open the page.** PTC-style ads, where the visitor opens one page at a time, keeps it visible and passes a check, are closer to real browsing than an unattended viewer.
