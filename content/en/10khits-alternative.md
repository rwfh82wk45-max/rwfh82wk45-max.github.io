10KHits is a traffic exchange that has been around since 2011. You earn points by viewing other members' sites and spend them on visits to yours. This page explains how it works and when an alternative might fit better. We are not affiliated with 10KHits; this site is published by the SurfFlux team.

## How 10KHits works

10KHits uses its own surfing software. You install it, it shows other members' sites, and each visit of about 10 seconds earns points. You then spend points on visits to your own pages and can set things like hits per hour and visit length.

According to independent overviews:

- New accounts get a small number of free points, which you can use after surfing a set number of pages.
- The free account covers the basics.
- A paid upgrade, listed at $29 per month, adds geo-targeting, a bounce rate reducer and custom traffic sources.
- Referrals earn extra points.

## Strengths

- Long track record for a traffic exchange.
- Lightweight software.
- Control over visit settings.

## Limits

- Fixed short visits push bounce rates up.
- Low conversion, as with all exchange traffic.
- Country targeting costs extra.
