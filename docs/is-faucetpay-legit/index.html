Is FaucetPay legit and safe?

By the SurfFlux team · Updated 2026-10-30

Short answer: FaucetPay is a real, working microwallet that a large part of the faucet and PTC world relies on, including SurfFlux, which pays its users through it. "Legit" and "safe for everything" are different questions, though, so here is the longer answer.

Is FaucetPay a scam or real?

FaucetPay is real. It processes small payments from a large number of earning sites and lets users withdraw them to their own wallets. Calling it a scam usually comes from confusing it with the sites that pay into it. A faucet that never pays does not make FaucetPay a scam, in the same way a bad shop does not make a bank a scam.

Watch out for look-alike domains, though. There are copycat sites with names close to FaucetPay's. Always type the address yourself or use a bookmark.

Is FaucetPay safe? The custodial part

FaucetPay is custodial. The coins in your balance are held by FaucetPay, not in a wallet where you control the keys. That has consequences:

  • If your account is restricted, you cannot move your coins until it is resolved.
  • If the service had a serious problem, balances held there would be at risk.

The sensible approach is the same as with any custodial service: use it for what it is good at, collecting small amounts, and withdraw to your own wallet regularly. Do not use it as savings.

Is the FaucetPay wallet safe from account theft?

Most lost balances are lost through phishing and reused passwords, not through hacks of the service. Turn on two-factor authentication, use a unique password, and never log in through a link someone sent you.

Is FaucetPay shutting down, or down right now?

Rumors that FaucetPay is shutting down appear from time to time, usually during outages. If the site is not loading, check FaucetPay's official announcement channels before you assume anything. Temporary downtime is not unusual for any service.

Is FaucetPay available in the USA?

Availability can depend on country and on the coins involved. Check FaucetPay's current terms for your country rather than relying on an old forum post.

Why sites like SurfFlux use FaucetPay

For a site that pays thousands of tiny rewards, sending each one on-chain is not practical. SurfFlux also uses the FaucetPay identity to stop people from running multiple accounts, which protects advertisers and honest users.
