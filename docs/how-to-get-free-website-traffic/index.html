How to get free website traffic

By the SurfFlux team · Updated 2026-10-30

Free website traffic costs time instead of money. The methods below are ordered roughly by how much lasting value they bring, from the slow-but-durable to the quick-but-shallow. Which one is best for you depends on whether you need visitors this week or customers next year.

1. Write pages that answer real searches

Search traffic is still the best free traffic there is, because the visitor came looking for what you wrote. Pick questions people actually type, answer them better than the current results, and give each page one clear topic. It takes months, but it keeps working.

2. Fix the basics so search engines can find you

Submit a sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, link your pages to each other, and make sure every page you care about is reachable within a few clicks from the home page.

3. Answer questions in communities

Forums, subreddits and Q&A sites where your audience already asks questions. Answer properly first and link only when your page genuinely helps. A good answer in an active thread can send visitors for years.

4. Get listed where your niche looks

Directories, "best of" lists and resource pages in your field. One relevant listing beats fifty generic directory links.

5. Ask for broken links to be replaced

Find pages in your niche that link to sites that no longer exist, and offer your page as a replacement. Site owners are often glad to fix a dead link.

6. Use social media without social media burnout

If you would rather not post daily, focus on one platform where your topic does well and repurpose each article into a few posts.

7. Email people who already like you

A small newsletter often sends more engaged visitors than a big social following.

8. Partner with similar sites

Guest articles, joint guides or simply linking to each other's best resources.

9. Traffic exchanges

Traffic exchanges give you free traffic quickly in return for your time: you view other members' sites and they view yours. The visits are short and the visitors are mostly other members, so do not expect sales. They are useful for testing pages or getting a first trickle of views. Read what a traffic exchange is before you start, and never send exchange visits to pages with AdSense.

How to get traffic to your website fast, for free

Of everything above, only communities and traffic exchanges are fast. Communities can bring engaged visitors within hours if your answer is good. Exchanges bring volume within minutes but little engagement. Everything else is slower and worth more.

Getting traffic but no sales?

That usually means the traffic does not match the page. Visitors from exchanges, giveaways or unrelated communities will not buy. Look at where your visitors come from before you change the page itself.
